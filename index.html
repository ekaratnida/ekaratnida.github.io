About

I received a Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) from Chulalongkorn University in 1999, an M.S. degree in Information Technology from King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT), Bangkok, Thailand, in 2003, and a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from National Chiao Tung University, Taiwan, in 2016, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Ying-Dar Lin. My research interests include mobile devices and systems, AI/ML, and video games.
